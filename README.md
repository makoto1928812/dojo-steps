# Dojo Steps

Flip the steps, copy the moves, earn your certificate.

- ▶ **Try it:** https://makoto1928812.github.io/dojo-steps/

## About

### Karate feels hard to start

If you know nothing about karate, it is hard to know where to begin. Dojo Steps is a simple first guide.

- Made for people who do not know karate
- A mini experience of 3 basic techniques
- Stance, straight punch, and front kick
- A Japanese dojo look: paper, ink, and red

### How Dojo Steps works

Pick a technique, flip through four step cards with Next, and copy each move. A master gives a tip on every card.

- Tap Next to flip each step card
- Each card has a picture, a short text, and a tip
- A master character gives one-line advice
- Switch between Japanese and English anytime

### A small win, then more

Finish all 3 techniques and the app shows a certificate. It is a fun end to a first try at karate.

- A progress bar shows how many are done
- The certificate has your name and a master's words
- You can print or save the certificate
- Next: decide what the certificate should say
